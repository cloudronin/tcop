## What is implemented

- A receiver-local zero-credit investigative-tip channel. A quarantined or
  restricted issuer can prompt bounded investigation only after its signed
  v0.2 observation and interaction receipt validate; its corroborative credit
  remains zero.
- Scoped provisional containment with an explicit TTL and confirmation
  deadline. Expiration is a deterministic de-escalation to monitoring and
  never a time-driven escalation.
- An authorized direct-local emergency path. It requires a local receiver,
  a registered observation/enforcement point scoped to the capability, signed
  `direct_local_authorized` metadata, and a signed
  `independent_audit_ref`. The original signed record, registry snapshot, and
  audit reference projected into the explanation all remain in the artifacts.
- Frozen-batch source-novel confirmation. A report cannot create and satisfy
  its own requirement in one virtual-time batch; repeats, relays, and later
  same-control-group interactions do not confirm by default.
- Deterministic evidence campaigns: compatible additions revise an existing
  campaign, the lexicographically smallest compatible ID is the canonical
  merge target, and incompatible scope, claim family, or time-window inputs
  create linked splits.
- A deterministic scheduler with global budget 3, a per-control-group cap of
  2, and a reserved high-risk slot. Low-risk tip floods therefore cannot
  consume high-risk investigation capacity.
- Separate machine-readable latency and damage measurements: campaign to
  provisional protection, tip to scheduled investigation, requirement to
  accepted confirmation, and damage in the inclusive requirement-to-deadline
  interval.

The local v0.4 records have independent schemas for tips, actions,
provisional responses, confirmation requirements and events, campaigns, and
response severity. CT-065–CT-090 cover the required conformance boundaries.

## Scenario findings

- **Staged protection prevents the authored immediate-collusion failure.** In
  B-056, the v0.3 weighted control fully quarantined all five receivers from
  the first batch of three false colluders. Full v0.4 created five provisional
  constraints, accepted no confirmation, and deterministically ended at
  monitoring after expiry.
- **Source novelty matters.** In B-058, full v0.4 rejected 15 repeated
  same-control-group confirmation attempts across the five receivers and did
  not quarantine. Disabling source novelty led to five confirmed quarantines.
  A new control group (B-059), failed independent patrol (B-061), and the
  authorized local point (B-060) each confirmed later genuine scenarios.
- **Direct-local authority is scoped and auditable.** B-060 includes a signed
  `audit-B-060-3` reference and a `runtime-sensor-node-1` scope registration.
  The receiving node that owns the observation point records
  `authorized_direct_emergency`; other receivers retain the original
  provenance without being allowed to relabel it as local.
- **Tips are useful but not a security bypass.** In B-051, five eligible,
  zero-credit tips caused provisional protection and later confirmation. In
  B-052, false tips produced no full quarantine and expired to monitoring. In
  B-055, an unavailable patrol intentionally produced no clean evidence: the
  scenario retained a missed warning and 700 milli-units of delay damage.
- **Capacity reservation resists the authored starvation case.** B-067
  generated 55 eligible tips across five receivers. Each receiver scheduled
  its financial-transfer tip through the reserved high-risk slot plus two
  low-risk actions; 40 low-priority actions were prevented by budget/cap
  rules in the aggregate.
- **Campaign timing is explicit.** B-064 revisions grouped adjacent-batch
  evidence into one campaign at every receiver. B-065’s later evidence lay
  outside the three-interval window and produced linked campaign splits.
- **Confirmation delay remains a real cost.** In B-063, the late patrol
  confirmed after two virtual intervals, while the authored attack accrued
  900 milli-units within the confirmation interval. This is an intentional
  negative result, not hidden by the final successful quarantine.
- **Architecture comparison uses common input.** B-070 records the same raw
  observations, receipt digests, patrol results, response-adapter interface,
  and declared central-service fault schedule for TCX and the central control.
  The distributed receivers confirmed the later independent source; the sole
  central decision loop became unavailable and ended monitored. The differing
  outcome is attributable to decision architecture, not a privileged input.

## Artifacts and reproduction

Run `make research-confirmation` for the isolated profile or `make research`
for all profiles. For every v0.4 run, the artifact directory contains immutable
evidence and receipt streams; patrol data; tips and scheduled actions;
provisional and confirmation records; campaign revisions/splits/merges;
severity projections; explanations; a separate benchmark-truth stream; and a
manifest that declares the input digests, direct-emergency registry, and fault
schedule. Targeted experiment results are placed below
`artifacts/confirmation-v0.4/experiments/`.

The profile decisions are in
[`spec/v0.4-confirmation-decisions.md`](../spec/v0.4-confirmation-decisions.md),
and the generated baseline matrix is
[`artifacts/confirmation-v0.4/confirmation-benchmark-report.md`](../artifacts/confirmation-v0.4/confirmation-benchmark-report.md).

## Limits

This remains a deterministic simulator with synthetic evidence, virtual time,
and simulated enforcement; it is not production authorization or incident
response. The independent audit reference is preserved and required as signed
evidence, but v0.4 does not integrate an external audit service or validate
the referenced system's contents. Capacity reservation is proven only for the
authored local scheduler and does not model a live shared work queue. The
central comparison isolates architecture under the declared fault but does not
prove distributed deployments are generally available or resistant to all
collusion strategies. No LLM, network service, production enforcement, or
Kubernetes component has been introduced.
