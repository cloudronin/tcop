## Study design

- Content-addressed inputs preserve five separate categories: authored
  environment facts; immutable signed observations and receipts;
  profile-independent scheduled outcomes; benchmark truth; and
  profile-derived records. Resolver outputs are never used as shared inputs.
- The feature registry declares all existing mechanisms, dependencies, policy
  parameters, persistent records, schemas, artifact streams, feature-tagged
  symbols, scenario families, and operator concepts before execution.
- The study executed P0–P7, nine protocol-invalid controls, 18 leave-one-out
  ablations, all I-01–I-10 interaction cells, and all 80 valid F1–F8
  combinations. Invalid combinations are rejected rather than scored.
- Instrumentation is post-decision only. The P7 parity record shows full v0.4
  behavior for B-051–B-070 and lower-layer native semantic backends for
  evidence acceptance, receipt/control-group classification, reliability, and
  pre-confirmation behavior. Instrumentation-enabled and disabled decision
  digests are equal.
- Complexity rules and integer C1–C3 cost weights are checked in. Shared code
  is attributed through deduplicated feature-tagged symbols, not entire shared
  source files.

The conflict between the security and utility indices is material. P2/P3 are
lower-complexity and score lower on this study's aggregate security index, but
carry substantially higher utility loss. P5/P6 reduce the utility index but
increase the aggregate security index in this heterogeneous corpus. P7 is
neither a universal Pareto winner nor a universally dominated security choice.

## Feature and profile disposition

The foundational signed-context, scope, freshness, replay, provenance, local
resolution, control-group, relay, and capability-response mechanisms remain
the core on invariant grounds. The study records interaction and leave-one-out
deltas for every non-foundational mechanism, plus separate preventive and
forensic/assurance dispositions.

Notably, the results do not support presenting a single universal deployment
tier. The frozen `minimal` (P2), `standard` (P5), and `high-assurance` (P7)
records are all marked `policy_dependent`. They remain valid, executed,
nested manifests for follow-on evaluation, but are not a claim that one tier is
best for every risk/utility cost model.

Mechanisms such as immutable provenance, interaction receipts, compromise
windows, accusation-cycle reporting, campaign grouping, active patrol, and the
direct-local emergency path retain explicit forensic or high-assurance value
even where their broad preventive delta is neutral or policy-sensitive.

## Integrity and negative results

- Every disabled feature has an activation proof with zero active invocation,
  state, transition, decision-contribution, and feature-artifact counts.
- `decision-change-attribution.jsonl` retains every P7 comparison and labels
  outcome-changing, exercised-but-neutral, and enabled-but-unexercised cases.
- Negative controls are retained as causal evidence but excluded from Pareto
  deployment selection.
- Seed-panel medians, minima, maxima, and disposition-change records are
  emitted. They are deterministic sensitivity results, not statistical
  estimates.
- The absence of a distinct selected tier is itself the key negative result;
  the study does not manufacture a smaller-profile conclusion.

## Limits

This study does not prove production sufficiency, observer honesty, or
universal minimality. It composes existing deterministic native behavior and
uses declared backend equivalence; it does not introduce a new generalized
resolver. M-015 reuses authored topology scenarios, while M-017 is explicitly
a deterministic analysis projection rather than a live performance benchmark.
No LLM, live agent, network service, production enforcement, or v0.6
federated-domain observer architecture has been added.
