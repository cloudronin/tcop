## Causal results

The strict counterfactual comparisons use identical captured action sequences,
Domain-B policy, receiver-local monitor, capability mapping, correlation
binding, strategy digest, and tool-service state. Only signed-context timing
changes.

| Treatment | Improved pairs | Unchanged pairs | Harmful-action delta |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Inside-window early | 24 | 20 | -72 |
| Inside-window boundary | 24 | 20 | -72 |
| Outside-window | 24 | 20 | -48 |
| Post-local-detection | 24 | 20 | -48 |
| Post-local-containment | 12 | 32 | -24 |

The 30 physical end-to-end arms give the clearest timing picture. A1 forwarded
30 harmful calls. Inside-window A2 blocked 30/30. Outside-window A2 forwarded
10 already-reached harmful calls and blocked 20 subsequent calls. Thus the
evidence supports prospective prevention for early context and does not claim
that late context reverses completed harm.

## Availability cost

RA-03 deliberately treats `repository.write` as a benign synthetic metadata
action. Across all six replay treatments, 360 benign calls were attempted;
320 forwarded and 40 were constrained. All 40 blocks occurred in the
inside-window early and boundary treatments (20 each). Workflow completion was
80/120 (66.7%). This is the measured availability cost of the broad
capability-specific local restriction, not a false-containment count alone.

## Amendment trail

The first two sealed live artifacts captured provider integration failures
before tool execution. They are retained as immutable negative results. The
subsequent amendments are scoped and documented:

1. `001`: uses the required `max_completion_tokens` provider field.
2. `002`: sets the required Chat Completions `reasoning_effort: none` mode for function tools.
3. `003`: aligns RA-03 eligibility with its already frozen benign metadata-write truth.
4. `004`: corrects completion-gate ordering in a trace-preserving successor.
5. `005`: reconciles the RA-03 derived utility counter from immutable replay rows.
6. `006`: adds the physical `tcopd-a` federation relay and replays frozen live traces through it without new model sampling.

No amendment changed protocol fields, policy, evidence classes, model prompts,
tool definitions, timing treatments, malicious eligibility, or the captured
live traces used for the final result.

## Scope

This validates a bounded synthetic reference architecture: the model generates
attempt traces, while all prevention decisions remain receiver-local. It is
not a claim of production-scale latency, real-world attack frequency, or
autonomous production enforcement.
