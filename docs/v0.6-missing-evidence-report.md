separate v0.6 analysis artifact; it does not alter the frozen federation

## Causal result

The strict A1/A2 audit emitted 177 candidate pairs. Fifty-four
containment-first pairs were input-equivalent: 30 reduced downstream harmful
actions, 24 were unchanged, and none worsened. They prevented 36 actions in
total, with benefits represented in every topology and no individual scenario
family accounting for more than one-third of the total.

The remaining 123 candidate pairs are deliberately excluded from the causal
comparison: the source matrix uses different local-policy configurations for
A1 versus the balanced and utility-preserving A2 strategies. The artifact
records this as `local_policy_configuration`, rather than treating it as a
protocol effect.

The supportable claim is therefore conditional: signed TCOP context can reduce
downstream harm when containment-first receiving-domain policy receives it
inside the measured action window. It is not a claim that every frozen policy
or every topology produces the same preventive benefit.

## Timing and comparator audit

The timing surface covers S01, S02, S03, S04, S11, and S17 across receipt
offsets -4 through +4, retaining infeasible-before-publication cells as such.
Containment-first prevents all harm through receipt offset 0 in the ordinary
scenarios; later receipt has no preventive effect. Balanced and
utility-preserving do not prevent harm in S01, S04, S11, or S17 under this
surface, while they have later conditional effectiveness in selected S02/S03
cells. The complete scenario-by-strategy thresholds are retained in the
artifact rather than collapsed into one aggregate boundary.

The A3 audit covered 54 matched source cells. Bounded fact sets were exactly
equal to the A2-exportable fact union. Six regressions were found: three
authority-placement effects and three central-outage effects. Policy-parity
and local-fallback diagnostics were executed for each regression, so none are
reported as an unqualified federation-versus-central advantage.

## Utility and forensics

Utility is measured from scheduled benign workload opportunities because the
frozen source event's `attempted` flag denotes adversarial intent and is false
for benign traffic. That source convention is retained explicitly in the raw
action records. Across the selected utility/negative-control cells,
containment-first restricted 18 of 18 opportunities, while balanced and
utility-preserving restricted 15 of 18. This is a material measured utility
cost, not a hidden or post-hoc score.

Forensic-oriented generated additional records but did not improve the frozen
rubric's reconstruction completeness relative to balanced (both scored
0.7778 in the matched scored cells). The evidence round therefore does not
claim a forensic-quality improvement merely from record volume.
