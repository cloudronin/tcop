# TC-RFC-0019: Weighted corroboration and collusion boundaries (Draft)

The local resolver SHALL record every influence component using deterministic
0–1000 fixed-point arithmetic: scope authority, receipt/evidence quality,
freshness, observer reliability, issuer-state factor, and independence.

Each control group has one capped contribution. A quarantined issuer has zero
active credit and a probationary issuer cannot independently meet a remote
quarantine threshold. Direct local protection remains local and scoped.
Results must not claim that weighting solves collusion. CT-052–CT-054, CT-060,
and B-033–B-035/B-043 map here.
