# TC-RFC-0023: Provisional protection and direct-local emergencies (Draft)

A qualifying remote first campaign SHALL receive only a scoped provisional
response under the default confirmation profile. Its response record SHALL
declare its deadline, expiry, maximum severity, and default de-escalation.
Expiry or elapsed time alone MUST NOT increase severity.

An exception is a direct-local emergency observation. It MUST name a registered
authorized observation or enforcement point for the observed scope, carry
signed authorization metadata, and carry a signed independent audit reference.
The receiver SHALL preserve both the registry decision and original evidence
provenance in its explanation and artifact stream. Remote evidence cannot be
relabelled as direct-local.

CT-071 through CT-074 and CT-080 through CT-081 cover these requirements.
