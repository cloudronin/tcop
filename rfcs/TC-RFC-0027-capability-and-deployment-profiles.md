# TC-RFC-0027: TCOP capability and deployment profiles (Draft)

TCOP deployment profiles SHALL be declarative compositions of already defined
mechanisms. A profile SHALL state enabled features, disabled features,
compatibility, and dependencies. The v0.5 study emits minimal, standard, and
high-assurance records only from executed configurations. A record MAY be
`not_distinct`, `policy_dependent`, or `deferred`; a tier name alone does not
imply universal deployment sufficiency.
