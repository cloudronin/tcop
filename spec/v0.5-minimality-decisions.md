# TCOP v0.5 deterministic minimality-study decisions

1. v0.5 is an analysis and composition layer only. It introduces no TCX wire
   field, observer, trust state, response state, evidence channel, patrol
   capability, or enforcement behavior.
2. v0.1 through v0.4 remain frozen. v0.5 rechecks their exact published
   digests in temporary roots and writes only beneath
   `artifacts/minimality-v0.5/`.
3. Scenario source records are content-addressed and partitioned into authored
   environment facts, immutable signed observations and receipts,
   profile-independent schedules, isolated benchmark truth, and
   profile-derived records. Resolver outputs are never shared input.
4. P0 through P7, negative controls, leave-one-out ablations, interaction
   cells, and F1--F8 combinations are declarative manifests. An invalid
   dependency combination is rejected before execution and is never scored.
5. P7 uses the existing full v0.4 confirmation behavior for B-051--B-070.
   Lower layers use their existing v0.1, v0.2, and v0.3 semantic backends for
   acceptance, receipt/control-group, reliability, and pre-confirmation
   behavior.
6. Instrumentation is read-only post-decision accounting. It has no resolver
   reference and enabled/disabled instrumentation must retain identical native
   decision digests, transitions, timing, and evidence acceptance.
7. A disabled feature emits zero activation, state, decision-contribution, and
   feature-artifact counts. Activation proofs distinguish disabled,
   enabled-but-unexercised, exercised-but-neutral, and outcome-changing paths.
8. Complexity rules are checked in before results. Shared code is attributed
   only through deduplicated feature-tagged symbols, never by assigning an
   entire imported module to every profile.
9. Canonical seed 42 and deterministic panel 101, 211, 503, and 997 are
   sensitivity executions, not estimates of real-world frequency.
10. Negative controls are protocol-invalid or architecture-violating causal
    controls. They are reported but excluded from deployment Pareto selection.
11. Every mechanism receives preventive and forensic/assurance dispositions.
    Candidate tiers are selected only from executed nested P2, P5, and P7
    configurations; a tier may be `not_distinct`, `policy_dependent`, or
    `deferred` rather than being artificially selected.
12. v0.5 ends at frozen profile manifests for B-001--B-070. Federated-domain
    observer architecture and v0.6 are explicitly out of scope.
